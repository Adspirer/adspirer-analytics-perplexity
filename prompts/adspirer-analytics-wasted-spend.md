# Wasted-spend review

Load adspirer-analytics-tools and adspirer-analytics-competitor-research.

1. Confirm the platforms, accounts and window (default: the last complete 30 days).
2. Run each platform's wasted-spend and search-term reads (see references/common-operations.md in
   adspirer-analytics-platform-reports).
3. Separate zero-conversion rows from high-CPA rows, and flag small samples and recent
   conversion lag before calling anything wasted.
4. Report the top items by spend with the evidence, the total at stake per currency, and a
   proposed action (negative keyword, exclusion, bid or budget change) for the user to apply in
   their own tools or a separate Adspirer connector.

Nothing is changed by this command.
